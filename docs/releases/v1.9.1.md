# Codex Usage Overlay 1.9.1

Show the Antigravity Argon limit next to 5H and WK.

The Antigravity overlay now reads **5H | WK | AR**: the 5-hour limit, the Gemini weekly limit and the Argon weekly (WTU) limit, each with its own reset countdown. The tray tooltip follows the same order (`Antigravity | 5H … | WK … | AR …`). It is the same for every quota pool:

- With the Gemini pool, WK is Gemini's weekly limit and AR is added after it.
- With the Argon pool, chosen in settings or picked by Auto for an Argon/orchestrator model, WK still shows Gemini's weekly limit and AR shows Argon's. The weekly burn rate is measured on the pool's own limit, so it appears under AR.
- With the 3P pool, WK is the 3P weekly limit and AR is added after it.

Only the display changed. Low-quota protection, notifications and burn rates keep acting on the selected pool's own limits. The tray icon's weekly ring also still follows the pool's own limit, so with the Argon pool it shows Argon while the tooltip's WK shows Gemini. The docked edge pill keeps its two bars.

## Validation and limits

Local Windows validation passed: the frontend production build and tests (14 files, 194 tests), the Rust test suite (511 passed, 9 existing ignored tests; antigravity and Claude bridges 10 and 11 passed), the DSH bridge tests (16/16), the packaging regressions and the affine parity fixture. New tests cover the Gemini, Argon and 3P pools, the tray tooltip and both overlay layouts. A development build was checked by hand against live Antigravity data with the Argon pool active and showed 5H, the Gemini weekly limit and AR together. The change was not checked in an installed copy; official signed NSIS, MSI and portable packages are built by the tagged Release workflow.

## 中文说明

在 Antigravity 的 5H 与 WK 旁显示 Argon 额度。

Antigravity 浮窗现在显示 **5H | WK | AR**：5 小时额度、Gemini 每周额度和 Argon 每周（WTU）额度，各自带有重置倒计时。托盘提示按相同顺序显示（`Antigravity | 5H … | WK … | AR …`）。各配额池的显示方式一致：

- Gemini 配额池：WK 为 Gemini 每周额度，其后显示 AR。
- Argon 配额池（在设置中选择，或使用 Argon/orchestrator 模型时由“自动”选中）：WK 仍显示 Gemini 每周额度，AR 显示 Argon 额度。每周消耗速率按配额池自身的额度计算，因此显示在 AR 下方。
- 3P 配额池：WK 为 3P 每周额度，其后显示 AR。

本次只改变显示方式。低额度保护、通知和消耗速率仍按所选配额池自身的额度计算。托盘图标的每周圆环同样仍跟随配额池自身的额度，因此在 Argon 配额池下圆环显示 Argon，而提示中的 WK 显示 Gemini。贴边收起时的胶囊仍为两条进度条。

本机 Windows 已通过前端生产构建与测试（14 个文件、194 项）、Rust 测试（511 项通过，9 项为既有忽略用例；Antigravity 与 Claude 桥接分别 10、11 项通过）、DSH 桥接测试（16/16）、打包回归及仿射一致性检查。新增测试覆盖 Gemini、Argon、3P 配额池、托盘提示及两种浮窗布局。已用开发版在实际 Antigravity 数据（Argon 配额池）下手动确认 5H、Gemini 每周额度与 AR 同时显示。未在安装版中验证；正式签名的 NSIS、MSI 与便携包由标签触发的 Release 工作流构建。
