# v1.4.1

This patch fixes intermittent “Quota read failed” warnings caused by valid Codex quota responses arriving after the old 20-second timeout. Local investigation observed valid responses taking 24–28 seconds.

- Quota refreshes now share a 45-second overall budget, including cleanup, with at most one retry for an early transient failure. Authentication and invalid-data failures do not retry inline.
- After transient recovery is exhausted, the next check starts 15 seconds later; consecutive failures back off through 30/60/120/240/300 seconds at the default interval. Explicit server retry delays are respected.
- Concurrent refresh callers receive the same success or failure. Cached values remain available for display without concealing a failed refresh.
- The 30-second protection freshness limit starts when the quota request is sent. Slow responses can update the overlay while remaining ineligible for pause/resume actions. Manual resume obtains quota and rechecks freshness and settings before sending.
- Settings shows reading/retry progress, the next recovery check, and the last failure and successful-read timing in English, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese.
- Local diagnostic history retains the latest success and failure independently and is bounded to 200 events, 256 KiB, and seven days. It records timings, error codes/categories, safe executable/version metadata, and counts of known stderr warnings; it does not record credentials, account/chat identifiers, raw RPC bodies, or raw helper stderr.

The underlying intermittent delay may come from the CLI, network, or service. A slow read still delays quota protection until usable data arrives. Existing cooperative stopping and assisted-resume limitations remain; this patch does not add direct interruption or automatic continuation capabilities.

Existing settings and burn-rate data remain compatible.

Validation: 355 Rust tests, 113 frontend tests, six packaging fixtures, 616 Edge rendering cases, and a read-only native quota-reader smoke run. Native WebView2/tray interaction, mixed-monitor behavior, and installer installation were not manually smoke-tested for this patch.
