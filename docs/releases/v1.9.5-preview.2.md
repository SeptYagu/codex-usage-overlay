# Codex Usage Overlay v1.9.5-preview.2

Fix preview on top of `v1.9.5-preview.1`. It keeps a free-floating overlay on screen after display changes and when it grows near a screen edge.

## Fixed

- **The overlay comes back on screen after a display change.** Adding or removing a monitor, changing its resolution or scaling, or moving the taskbar now puts a free-floating overlay back at its saved spot (where it was last dragged), fitted inside that screen's work area. If that screen is gone, the overlay lands on the nearest one and moves back once the screen returns. Before, it could stay off screen until it was hidden and shown again from the tray. A docked pill is re-placed on these changes too, instead of waiting for the next usage refresh.
- **A free-floating overlay near the right or bottom edge no longer gets cut off when it grows.** The window keeps fitting its content, and growing used to push it past the screen edge, for example when the provider carousel switched to a provider with wider readings. It now shifts left or up just enough to stay on screen, and goes back to its saved spot when it shrinks again.

## Changed

- With the provider carousel on, an overlay dragged against the right edge now keeps its right edge on the screen edge as providers rotate, so it shifts left and right instead of spilling past the edge. An overlay that was never dragged keeps its default margin from the right edge the same way.
- The saved spot still changes only when you drag the overlay. Moving it to fit a screen never overwrites it.

## Known limits

- The known limits listed for `v1.9.5-preview.1` still apply.

## 中文说明

基于 `v1.9.5-preview.1` 的修复预览版：显示器变化后，以及在屏幕边缘附近变大时，自由悬浮的悬浮窗都会留在屏幕内。

### 修复

- **显示器变化后悬浮窗会回到屏幕内。** 增减显示器、修改分辨率或缩放、移动任务栏后，自由悬浮的悬浮窗会回到保存的位置（最后一次拖拽到的地方），并收进该屏幕的工作区。该屏幕不在了就落到最近的屏幕，屏幕接回后再回到原处。此前它可能一直留在屏幕外，直到从托盘隐藏再显示。停靠的小浮窗也会在这些变化后立即重新落位，不再等下一次用量刷新。
- **靠近右侧或底部边缘的自由悬浮窗变大时不再被裁掉。** 窗口尺寸跟随内容，此前变大会把窗口推出屏幕边缘，例如轮播切换到读数更宽的 provider 时。现在它只会左移或上移刚好留在屏幕内的距离，变小后回到保存的位置。

### 变更

- 开启 provider 轮播时，拖到右边缘的悬浮窗随轮播切换会保持右边缘贴着屏幕边缘，因此会左右移动，而不是伸出屏幕。从未拖拽过的悬浮窗同样保持默认的右边距。
- 保存的位置仍然只在拖拽时改变；为适应屏幕而移动窗口不会覆盖它。

### 已知限制

- `v1.9.5-preview.1` 列出的已知限制仍然适用。
